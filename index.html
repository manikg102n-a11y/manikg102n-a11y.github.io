How it works

Choose a tournament

Pick Solo, Duo or Squad, and the entry fee and time that suit you.

Register

Enter your in-game name and Free Fire UID (and your partner or teammates).

Pay the entry fee

Pay by UPI and enter the UPI ID you paid from. Your registration stays pending until an admin verifies the payment.

Get the room ID

Room ID and password go to you 15 minutes before start.

Rules

Match rules

  • No team up
  • No panel use
  • No hack use
  • No gun attributes

Prize rules

  • No prize for hack or panel users
  • Pay per kill available
  • Every match is recorded and kept for any consequences, such as disputes or penalties

Scoring

Placement points plus 1 point per kill across 3 matches. Highest total wins. Ties go to most Booyahs.

Be on time

Join the custom room within 10 minutes of the room ID being shared. Late entries lose their slot.

Questions

How do I get my money back?

If a tournament is cancelled or doesn’t fill, every entry fee is refunded. Fees are not refunded after the room ID has been shared.

How are prizes paid?

Winners receive their prize by UPI within 24 hours of the final match, after a quick check of results.

What is the difference between the modes?

Battle Royale: Solo is one player per slot, Duo is a pair, Squad is a team of 4. Clash Squad and Lone Wolf are fast matches where two teams face each other, in 1v1, 2v2 or 4v4 (Lone Wolf is 1v1 or 2v2).

Do I need to be a certain level?

You need Free Fire level 40 or higher, and the UID you register must be the one you play with.
